    Rates & what we shoot

    Live Action, trap and skeet. Open to the public Saturdays and Sundays, and to members Monday through Friday. Walk on and shoot — call ahead if you are bringing a group.

    What we shoot

    Three stands, all of them weekend-only. Pricing is set at the clubhouse — call and we will tell you exactly what your round costs before you drive out.

    At the clubhouse

    Ammunition is sold here, so you do not have to haul a case of shells across the county to shoot a round. Snacks and cold drinks too — an Alabama Saturday on an open field earns both.

    Check in at the clubhouse before you head out to a stand.

    Bring your own gun

    Bring one that works and that fits you. Any shotgun in safe working order is welcome — see the gun and ammunition rules before you load up.

    Groups & events

    Bringing a crowd — a company outing, a fundraiser, a birthday, a bachelor party? Call ahead so we can have the stands and the machines ready for you rather than fitting you in around a busy Saturday.

    Call (205) 821-4879 to talk it through.

    Before you come

    • Open to the public Saturdays and Sundays. Monday to Friday is members only.
    • Bring your own shotgun.
    • Ammunition, snacks and drinks are available at the clubhouse.
    • Eye and ear protection is required in the shooting area.
    • Cash and card both accepted at the clubhouse.
    • Call ahead if you are bringing a group.
    • We shoot in rain and cold. Lightning closes the stands.
